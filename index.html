Click a Cookie is the game I am focused on most right now. The idea is simple: click for cookies, buy upgrades and rebirth for a bigger multiplier. There are different cookies to find, another area on the moon and an inventory for the ones you want to keep. The game is playable, but I am still changing a lot.

What is in the game

  • 01Clicking and upgradesEarn cookies and spend them on better upgrades.
  • 02RebirthsStart again with a multiplier and build up faster.
  • 03The moonA second area with mutations you cannot get from normal clicks.
  • 04Cookie collectionFind Sparkle, Rainbow and Huge versions of cookies.
  • 05Inventory and rewardsKeep, sell or drop your finds. Playing with two friends gives you a Rainbow Cookie.
